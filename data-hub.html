Club data nervous system

Feed, store, transmute and receive the right data

Google Drive can become the private club operations vault. GitHub Pages stays the public-safe interface. Agents, Sheets and exports sit between them.

Core pattern

Raw data goes in privately. Approved meaning comes out publicly.

This keeps members, team locations, payments and private media protected while still letting the club produce fast committee views, public stories and grant-ready evidence.

01

Feed

Forms, Gmail, Drive uploads, meeting files, Stripe exports, field notes, media and agent snapshots.

02

Store

Private Drive folders, Sheets ledgers, permission registers, media review queues and audit notes.

03

Transmute

Agents turn raw files into member status, action lists, field summaries, public-safe map zones and grant evidence.

04

Receive

Stakeholders receive the right view: member notices, committee dashboards, treasurer exports, safety summaries or public stories.

Stakeholder routing

Each person gets a different window into the same system

The same raw event can become different outputs depending on role, permission and urgency.

Members

Renewal status, comp entry confirmations, newsletters, event reminders and permission requests.

Secretary

Member register, meeting pack, agenda intake, action register and public communication drafts.

Treasurer

Stripe reconciliation, deposits, product exports, fee reports and fundraiser summaries.

Field leads

Event check-ins, broad location logs, safety notes, media queue and condition snapshots.

Media leads

Approved photos, private media queue, wrap-up drafts, consent status and sponsor-safe captions.

Public

Approved stories, broad map zones, event outcomes, conservation work and club invitations.

Google Calendar queue

Calendar should be the action layer, not the source of truth

Sheets hold the draft and approval state. Calendar carries the approved time, owner, reminder pattern and invite group. Gmail sends the trail.

Competitions

Private calendar event for organisers and members, public export only after broad details and media are approved.

Committee meetings

Agenda, recordings and transcripts stay private until minutes or public updates are explicitly approved.

Working bees

Volunteer callouts can be public, but contact details, attendance notes and task assignments stay inside the vault.

Renewals

Calendar drives campaign timing while individual member renewal status remains private in Sheets.

Content publishing

Posts move from media queue to scheduled output only after permission, caption and sponsor checks.

Grants and sponsors

Deadline reminders keep evidence moving without exposing negotiation notes, budgets or drafts.

Agent handoff

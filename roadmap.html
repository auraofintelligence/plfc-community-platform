Next actions

A sensible sequence from prototype to demonstration

Each step is small enough to explain, approve and improve without making the committee feel trapped by a giant tech project.

  1. Approve the story spine. Confirm the homepage language, member promise, conservation wording and community tone.
  2. Confirm the fee table. Verify membership, casual comp, supporter, fundraiser and merch prices before any Stripe item goes live.
  3. Choose the first live payment path. Start with one or two Stripe Payment Links, then add Checkout Sessions when form data needs to be passed through.
  4. Design the member form. Decide required fields, optional fields, privacy wording, newsletter consent and SMS consent.
  5. Pilot the meeting pack flow. Use one committee meeting to test agenda intake, report capture, minutes and action tracking.
  6. Publish the open-source repo. Use GitHub Pages for the prototype and keep sensitive documents out of the public repo.
  7. Scope the field-ops layer. Confirm tide, weather, GPS, upload, media-permission and digital-twin boundaries before collecting live field data.
  8. Set up the Google Drive vault. Create private Drive folders, Sheets ledgers and public-safe export rules before any agent reads club data.
